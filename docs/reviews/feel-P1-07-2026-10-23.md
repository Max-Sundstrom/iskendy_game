# Feel review #1 (P1-07) — provisional, from numbers and bots (2026-10-23)

Reviewer: game-design critic role, played by the Orchestrator session. **This review has not been felt by a person.**
It reads the tuning, the sim and the bot dashboard (P1-08). The P1 exit gate ("a first-time player understands what to
do within two attempts") needs a human playtest; the checklist at the end is for the owner.

## What the numbers say

| Topic | Now | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Lane change | 0.16 s, ease-out cubic; ≥ half the way after 3 ticks | Snappy: at 35 m/s the change spans 5.6 m of road, at 95 m/s 15 m. No change proposed |
| Jump arc | apex 4.2 m at 0.45 s, fall ×1.6 → 0.81 s airtime | 28 m of road at 35 m/s (about 1.5 rows), 77 m at 95 m/s (6–7 rows at 11 m spacing). Late jumps carry the car over traffic it hasn't seen yet. Watch in P2 with the flips |
| Fly | holds −1 m/s after easing from the jump; peak ≈ 5.6 m | Clears semis (4.0 m); 600 L lasts 10 s of pure flight. Fine for P1 |
| Speed perception | FOV 62° → 74° with speed; only lane dashes move | The grey box has nothing at the roadside, so 35 m/s reads slow. Lamp posts or kerb props (P3-03) are needed for speed to feel like speed |
| Difficulty | Novice median 498 m (target ≥ 600 m), nobody past ≈ 850 m | Too hard too early, and not yet fair: no solver (P2-06), and long vehicles arrive at 500–800 m |
| Scrapes | half of Novice's deaths are second scrapes within 4 s | Cutting in is punished hard for a grey box without indicators or near-miss feedback |

## Proposed tuning deltas (apply after the playtest confirms the reading)

| Key | From | To | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sim.vehicles[semi].fromM` | 500 | 1 200 | Long blockers before the player has learned roofs/fly; the fairness solver is not there yet |
| `sim.vehicles[bus].fromM`, `[trolleybus].fromM` | 800 | 1 500 | same |
| `sim.collision.secondScrapeWindowS` | 4.0 | 3.0 | Keeps the stumble rule but forgives a second mistake sooner; revisit with near-miss feedback (P2-03) |
| `sim.director.firstRowM` | 80 | 120 | A breath of open road while the player reads the controls hint (tutorial comes in P4-03) |

Expected effect, to verify on the dashboard: Novice median ≥ 600 m and some runs past 1 km. The goldens and the
determinism hash change with any of these; log them in the sim changelog.

## Owner playtest (10 minutes, phone and laptop)

1. Open the review page, tap «Играть». Don't read the hint first — does the screen make it clear what to do?
2. Five runs. After each, note: distance, what killed you, and whether it felt fair.
3. Try once each: hold the swipe up (fly), swipe down in the air (slam), hold BOOST.
4. Rate 1–5: lane snappiness, jump height and length, sense of speed.
5. Send the `p95` line from «Играть с замером» and the phone model.
