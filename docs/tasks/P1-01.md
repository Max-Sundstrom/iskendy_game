# P1-01 — Sim core: state, fixed step, RNG, lanes, speed curve

Owner: sim-engineer · Reviewers: qa-engineer, code-reviewer · Phase: 1 · Depends on: P0-01, P0-03

## Goal
A deterministic simulation skeleton that moves the player forward along the speed curve and switches lanes.

## Context
GDD §5.1–5.2 · ARCHITECTURE §3, §4.1–4.4 · tuning keys `sim.tickRateHz`, `sim.lanes.*`, `sim.speed.minMps`,
`sim.speed.maxMps`, `sim.speed.distanceScaleM`, `sim.world.originShiftM`.

## Scope
`packages/sim/src/**`, `packages/sim/test/**`, `packages/shared/src/sim/**` (contract types only).

## Out of scope
Jumping, traffic, scoring, rendering.

## Contract (frozen before parallel work: yes)
- `createSim(seed, tuning): SimState`
- `step(state, actions: ActionBits): void` — mutates in place, clears and refills `state.events`
- `snapshot(state, out: SimSnapshot): void` — copies into a preallocated read-only view
- `hashState(state): string` — FNV-1a over quantised fields
- Events used: `laneChange`, `originShift`

## Acceptance criteria
1. Speed at distance d matches `v(d)` from GDD §5.2 within 0.5 % at d = 0, 1 000, 4 000, 10 000 m (LUT-based).
2. Lane change completes in `changeDurationS` ± 1 tick; one extra input is queued; lanes clamp at the edges.
3. OriginShift fires every `originShiftM`; player z re-bases; distance-travelled stat is unaffected.
4. 10 000 ticks with a fixed seed and scripted inputs produce the same hash in Node and in Chromium (Playwright page).
5. No forbidden APIs in packages/sim (dependency-cruiser + a grep test for Math.sin/cos/exp/pow/random, Date, performance).
6. `step()` allocates nothing (heap snapshot diff over 10 000 ticks < 1 KB).
