# P1-08 — Synthetic players v0 (Novice, Chaos) running nightly
Owner: QA + Sim (done by the Orchestrator session) · Reviewers: game-design-critic · Phase: 1 · Depends on: P1-03

## Goal
A balance dashboard from day one: bots play the real sim through the same Action interface as people, every night.

## Context
TEST_STRATEGY §1, §3; ADR-014; ARCHITECTURE §17.

## Scope
`tools/bots/{policies,run,cli}.ts` + tests, `pnpm bots`, `.github/workflows/nightly.yml`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Novice (350 ms reaction, ground only, 10 % input errors) and Chaos (random inputs) run nightly.
2. A dashboard with the distance distribution: histogram, median/p10/p90, crash causes, anomalies.

## Handoff note
- **What changed:**
  - policies read the state and press actions with their own seeded RNG (never the sim's);
  - Novice queues decisions behind a 21-tick reaction delay; Chaos holds random masks for 1–40 ticks;
  - the runner checks every tick for non-finite state and caps runs at 10 minutes;
  - the report goes to `bots-report/report.{md,json}`, and the nightly job writes it to the job summary and uploads
    it as an artifact;
  - anomalies fail the run; balance alerts are reported, not fatal, until P2 tunes the game.
- **Verified:** 97 tests (bots deterministic per seed; Chaos: 300 runs with no anomalies or stuck runs; Novice
  outlives Chaos). Local 200-run report:
  - Novice median 498 m (p10 265, p90 693, max 847); deaths: 102 rear, 98 second scrape;
  - Chaos median 358 m (max 886).
- **Balance findings, for P1-07 and P2:**
  - Novice is under the 600 m target, and nobody gets past ~850 m. Semis (from 500 m) and buses (from 800 m) block
    lanes for long stretches, and director v0 has no fairness solver yet (P2-06).
  - Half of Novice's deaths are second scrapes: cutting into neighbours. Worth a look at the 4 s window or at
    telegraphing in P2-10.
- **Follow-ups:**
  - P2-11 adds Average and Expert, mutation testing and balance alerts against targets;
  - the dodging policy in `tools/make-golden-replays.ts` can move into `tools/bots`.
