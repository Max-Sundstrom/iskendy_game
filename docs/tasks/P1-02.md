# P1-02 — Jump, gravity, fly, slam, boost, AIR
Owner: Sim engineer (done by the Orchestrator session) · Reviewers: code-reviewer, qa-engineer · Phase: 1 · Depends on: P1-01

## Goal
The car leaves the ground: jumps with a snappy arc, flies on thrusters, slams down and boosts, all paid for from the
AIR tank.

## Context
GDD §4 (input feel: buffer, hold-to-fly), §5.3–5.5, §6 (AIR); scenarios G3, G5; tuning `sim.jump.*`, `sim.fly.*`,
`sim.slam.*`, `sim.air.*`, `sim.speed.boostMultiplier`.

## Scope
`packages/sim/src/{lateral,vertical,state,step,hash,events}.ts` + tests, `packages/shared/src/{events.ts,tuning/schema.ts}`,
`packages/content/tuning.default.json` (one key), DECISIONS (ADR-024, sim changelog).

## Contract
New state fields: `y, vy, airborne, flying, slamming, boosting, jumpHeldS, jumpBufferS, airLiters`. New event codes
5–10: `jump, flyStart, flyStop(reason), slam, land(slam), airEmpty`. New tuning key `sim.fly.glideEaseS`. Frozen: no.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Apex height and time match tuning within 1 %.
2. AIR rates (fly, boost, stacked, regen) match tuning within 1 %.
3. G3 jump buffer and G5 running dry behave as specified.

## Handoff note
- **What changed:**
  - **Jump:** exact constant-gravity integration within each step, with rising and falling gravity.
  - **Fly:** gravity off, vertical speed eases to −1 m/s with time constant `glideEaseS`, ceiling 9 m. Over
    semis it rises to about 5.6 m, then glides down.
  - **Slam:** constant −30 m/s, cancels flight, landing reports `slam`.
  - **Boost:** ×1.25 while AIR lasts.
  - **AIR:** regen only on the ground and not boosting; costs stack; `airEmpty` sent once.
  - Lane logic moved to `lateral.ts`.
