## Selected rules and reasons

- R1: active AIR is free while the finite tank is preserved, with no passive regeneration or consumption.
  Flight and BOOST may start at stored zero. Grace resumes ordinary costs and regeneration. Never store Infinity.
- R2: an actual active contact destroys the vehicle exactly once, including a currently ridden platform.
  No HOP, new support, artificial landing, SLAM credit or coyote launch is created by its destruction.
  A jump already launched from that platform remains valid. Actual later road landings retain their own awards.
- R3: actual touchdowns during active or grace align to STUNT, including untricked landings. No award
  without a real touchdown. After grace, ordinary angle grading applies; no protection-until-landing tail.
  Grace phases through traffic: no new HOP/platform support/roof SLAM reward, consistent with design §3.
  Existing support detaches without coyote/landing; grace does not destroy that object before final cleanup.
  Road touchdown remains a real landing. A jump launched from support before its closed t=0 active contact
  may destroy the former support, but its launch velocity/airborne state must survive that removal.
- R4: all protection lasts exactly the active interval plus grace. On the final protected tick, retire
  remaining end-position closed-AABB overlaps/touches without SMASH, score, meter or CLEAR/NEAR awards.
  This removes a trapped obstacle without teleporting the player or leaving an indefinitely immune object.
  It is a new selected rule replacing PR #9's proposed until-exit tail. Other vehicles remain ordinary hazards.
- R5: SMASH chain resets at each new active episode. Each real destruction increases chain once; award
  min(smashCap, smashBase + smashChainStep * (chain - 1)) through the existing style pot/N rules, meter +0.
  Mode expiry neither banks nor loses the existing pot. Ordinary loss/timeout priorities are unchanged.
- Stored full meter is not burned during this first active/grace slice. Trigger only if the tick STARTED idle,
  autoTrigger is true, the run survived the tick and meter is full after scoring. Consume once, start for the
  next physics tick. Consequently grace must be followed by one ordinary physics tick before a new earned
  mode. This bounds continuous protection without an invented cooldown number; it is not a balance claim.
  The next OVERDRIVE card will add one paid extension per episode, then retain later fill under this rule.

## Frozen contracts and ordering

Shared numeric contracts append without renumbering: ModeKind none=0/liftoff=1; ScoreReason.smash=12;
SimEventKind modeStarted=27, modeEnded=28, modeGraceEnded=29, smash=30, trafficPhasedOut=31.
modeStarted a=ModeKind, b=active ticks; modeEnded a=ModeKind, b=grace ticks; modeGraceEnded unused payload;
smash a=vehicle type, b=episode chain count; trafficPhasedOut a=vehicle type. No event dereferences a slot
after director reuse. Decoded union is fixed in shared/events.ts by the orchestrator.

SimState.mode is a preallocated object containing kind, activeTicksLeft, graceTicksLeft and smashChain.
Reuse the P2-04a1 clock functions; active/grace phases are read at the beginning of the physics tick.
Hash every persistent field, representing safe-integer tick counters without losing their upper 32 bits.
Do not store wall clocks, Infinity, callbacks, object-tail immunity or per-step allocations.

Order: capture phase → existing input/movement/AIR/vertical/speed → move traffic/ride/contact resolution →
near-miss finish → scoring → advance the OLD phase or start from an originally idle phase → director → origin.
New mode and grace counters do not lose a tick on creation. End-of-tick score cannot rescue a same-tick crash.
Active speed multiplier multiplies existing BOOST and penalty factors; neither penalty history nor inputs reset.

Within contact resolution: actual active contact/SMASH has priority, including the last active tick. For a
remaining object, final protected-tick closed end-overlap cleanup precedes CLEAR/NEAR producers. This also
applies when configured grace is zero: activeEnded goes directly idle without a spurious graceEnded event.
Do not retrospectively delete emitted events. Objects already fully separated at tick end are not retired.
Protected contacts invalidate pending CLEAR/NEAR encounters; unrelated contact-free awards remain valid.
Destroy/retire clears active/over/near state and detaches support without new coyote/landing. Spawn initializes
tracking before marking active, so immediate slot reuse cannot inherit any reward or protection state.
Update the event-buffer bound and score credit aggregation for the new reason; do not silently drop events.

Mode contacts use closed swept AABB in x/y/relative-z with ONE common interval of time, no epsilon or
substeps. Face/roof touching counts as protected contact. Strictly above the roof throughout the common
x/z overlap remains contact-free. Keep ordinary-mode collision rules unchanged in this card; do not reuse
their endpoint-only lateral/height gates for protected contact. The strict finite-expiry policy addresses
custom/future equal-speed overlap as well as current geometry; no infinite default-tuning exploit is claimed.
