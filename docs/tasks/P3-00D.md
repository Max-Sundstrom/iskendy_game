## Acceptance criteria

1. A fresh production build starts through the normal C intro, loads its assets under strict local CSP and shows
   the combined modern vehicle model in a mobile viewport.
2. A real jump and airborne left input visibly rotate the mesh through a committed full barrel and land safely.
3. A natural collision shows its cue/effect, preserves one hidden frame, resumes to results and records a valid replay.
4. One Retry follows the short intro and reaches a fresh grounded run with reset pitch and roll.
5. Evidence includes nine actual screenshots, raw results, exact source/runtime versions, earlier failure and
   unrun checks. Independent QA inspects the report and images.
6. The publication record identifies the parent's preview URL and source without claiming hosted validation
   that was not performed. Leave runtime/build/site files unchanged during this follow-up.
