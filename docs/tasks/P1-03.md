## Goal
Something to dodge: rows of traffic ahead, and contacts that end or punish the run exactly as GDD §5.9 says.

`sim.director.*`, `sim.collision.*`, `sim.speed.scrapePenalty*`, `sim.roofs.hopHeightM/landingToleranceM`.

## Scope
`packages/sim/src/{traffic,director,collisions,lanes,lateral,state,step,hash,events,testing}.ts` + tests, schema,
tuning JSON, `apps/web/test/determinism.ts`, `e2e/determinism.spec.ts`, ARCHITECTURE §4.5, DECISIONS changelog.

## Contract
- **State:** `traffic` (SoA, capacity 64, z = rear bumper), `director.nextRowM`; player gains `laneStartX`,
  `penaltyS`, `scrapeTimerS`.
- **Events:** `hop` 11, `scrape` 12, `crash(cause)` 13.
- **Vehicles** live in `sim.vehicles` (tuning, hashed).
- Frozen: no.

## Acceptance criteria
1. No tunnelling at 95 m/s in a 100k-step fuzz test.
2. A rear hit crashes; side contact during a lane change scrapes (revert, −20 % speed for 1 s, −40 L AIR); a second
   scrape within 4 s crashes.
3. Rows spawn ahead with at least one free lane; vehicles in a lane never overlap (G13).

## Handoff note
- **What changed:**
  - **Traffic:** SoA with car-following, so a faster vehicle settles behind a slower one.
  - **Director v0:** rows every 18 → 11 m as difficulty ramps over 8 km; 1–4 blocked lanes; only `constant` and
    `slow` vehicles until P2-06; respects `fromM`.
  - **Collisions:** swept on z using start and end positions; a car roof from above gives a hop (platform riding
    comes in P2-01); a scrape eases back from the current x (`laneStartX`).
  - **Schema rule:** every vehicle is slower than `minMps × (1 − scrapePenalty)`, so nothing can hit the player
    from behind.
  - **Determinism check:** a 10 000-tick session that restarts after crashes and folds the hash every 60 ticks.
- **Verified:**
  - all checks green, 72 tests: 100 000 fuzzed steps at ≈ 95 m/s (118 m/s boosted) with no overlap; G13 property
    over 25 seeds × 30 s of dense traffic; rear crash, jump over a sedan, scrape → revert → second-scrape crash,
    roof hop;
  - Chromium determinism E2E with golden `7083bcea-3e3cf928`; tuningHash `38343d24…` cross-checked in Python.
- **Known gap:** with today's vehicles (≥ 3.9 m) and ≤ 1.7 m relative motion per step, an end-of-step test alone
