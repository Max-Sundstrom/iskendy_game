# P2-02 — Front flips, barrel rolls, landing grades
Owner: Sim engineer (done by the Orchestrator) · Reviewers: qa-engineer, game-design-critic · Phase: 2 · Depends on: P1-02

## Goal
Tricks in the air (GDD §5.6–5.7): a second Jump press while airborne flips the car nose-over (the owner asked for it on
2026-10-23, citing the roadster video), and the angle at touchdown grades the landing.

## Context
GDD §4, §5.6, §5.7 · scenarios G3, G6 · tuning `sim.tricks`, `sim.landing`, `sim.speed.sloppyPenalty*`.

## Scope
`packages/sim/src/{tricks,outcome,vertical,roofs,state,hash,events}.ts` and tests; the renderer reads `flipTurns`.

## Out of scope
Barrel rolls on airborne lane changes (part 2 of this card), points and the streak (P2-03), mode auto-righting (P2-04).

## Contract
New events: `flipStart` (17), `flipDone` (18), `landingGraded` (19); crash cause 3 is `landing`. Player state gains
`flipTurns`, `flipping`, `flipQueued`, `flipsDone` and `penaltyFactor`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Jump pressed again in the air starts a committed 360° flip in 0.55 s; a press during a flip queues one more.
2. Grades: ≤ 12° PERFECT, ≤ 35° STUNT, ≤ 75° SLOPPY (−15 % for 0.8 s), worse crashes; bounds inclusive (G6).
3. G3 still holds: a press when the road is less than the 120 ms buffer away is a buffered jump, not a flip.
4. Hops and platform landings are graded too.

## Verification
`pnpm test` (tricks.test.ts); the renderer turns the car with `flipTurns`.
