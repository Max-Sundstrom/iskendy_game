# P2-02b — Committed barrel rolls and combined landing grades

Owner: sim engineer · Reviewers: independent QA, render reviewer · Phase: 2 · Depends on: P2-02 part 1, P3-00C

## Goal

An accepted lane change while airborne starts a committed 360° barrel roll toward that lane in 0.45 s.
Road, hop-roof and platform touchdowns grade the actual combined front-flip/barrel orientation.

## Context and scope

GDD §4–5.8; scenarios G1–G6/G11; ADR-024; AGENTS invariants 2/3/10 and §14.
This branch builds on the crash presentation checkpoint, separately from vehicle model refinement.
Own sim/shared contracts, sim tuning, math table generator, fixtures/goldens and task-specific tests/docs.
After sim contracts are implemented, renderer/session/crash-pose consumers and browser tests are in scope.
No scoring, new models, dependencies, deployment, four deferred tuning proposals or ornament changes.

## Frozen contract

- Start only inside successful `startLaneChange` when already airborne. A queued lane change starts its roll
  when it actually begins; lane bumps, merely queued input and collision auto-return do not start a roll.
  Existing lateral-before-vertical order stays: simultaneous ground Jump+lane does not roll.
- Player gains `rollTurns: number` (signed; right positive), `rollTargetTurns: number` (integer),
  `rolling: boolean`, `rollQueuedDir: number` (-1/0/1), `rollsDone: number` (count, not absolute/net angle).
  Every field participates in the state hash. Active rotation never reverses or cancels on another input.
- Append events `rollStart:20` (`a` next index, `b` direction) and `rollDone:21` (`a` completed count,
  `b` direction). Decoded variants carry `index`/`count` and `direction: -1 | 1` respectively.
  Existing `landingGraded.b` continues to mean completed flips. Never renumber existing events.
- `sim.tricks.barrelRollQueuedInputs` is 0 or 1, default 1: the first accepted lane change during a roll
  queues one next roll; overflow does not alter the roll but normal lane motion continues. This proposed
  product default must be recorded in DECISIONS/GDD, with flag 0 covered independently.
- `sim.landing.orientationMode` is `uprightForward` (default) or `upVector`. The default grades quaternion
  distance from upright AND forward: abs(cos(pitch/2)*cos(roll/2)). It rejects 180° pitch + 180° roll, which
  leaves wheels downward but the nose backward. The alternate grades cos(pitch)*cos(roll), the up-vector
  tilt only. Record this explicit GDD ambiguity/default in DECISIONS; do not silently decide it.
- Compare deterministic checked-in cosine table values; no transcendental Math calls in sim. Preserve
  inclusive pure-axis ±12/35/75° assertions and verify mixed axes against an independent test-only oracle.
- Complete the default roll on tick 27 exactly, clamp to its exact integer target, and begin a queued roll
  without advancing it twice in that tick. Bound any numerical epsilon; do not delay completion to tick 28.
- Clear active/queued trick work on landing/crash/retry. Safe landing resets angles and counts. A landing
  crash preserves actual terminal pitch/roll for the frozen crash visual, removing the preceding-tick
  workaround from P3-00C. Explain this intentional hashed-state change in the Sim changelog.
- Renderer interpolates signed roll without wrapping away turns, composes Qx(-pitch) * Qz(-roll), and
  compensates the rotated COM in all three axes. Sim remains the only source of outcome/orientation.

## Acceptance and verification

1. Both directions finish exactly at 360° after 27 ticks; chained/opposite rolls keep committed directions,
   correct events/counts and bounded queue state. Ground/edge/both-input/scrape cases do not create false rolls.
2. Road, hop and platform grades include both axes. Safe G11 roofs hop; an unsafe rotation crashes. Include
   pure-axis thresholds ±1° and exact bounds, 25°/-25°, 8.5°/8.5°, mixed signs and whole-turn periodicity.
3. Independent tests verify preserved terminal pose, interrupted/queued/repeated input, Retry and replay
   round-trip. Existing assertions remain unless a changed outcome is specifically justified and reviewed.
4. Update golden replays, BOTH 10k determinism fixtures and the Sim changelog with before/after hashes and
   outcome rationale. Keep SIM_VERSION 1 only under prerelease ADR-024; cross-engine CI must match.
5. Actual keyboard/touch-emulated browser scenarios show full roll and safe/unsafe touchdown. Inspect real
   pixel captures at both viewports; a DOM angle is not visual proof. Report physical-phone and local
   Firefox/WebKit limitations. Run typecheck/lint/unit/sim/build/size plus perf and applicable CSP checks.
6. Publish a separate draft PR based on the crash branch. No automatic merge or public deployment.
