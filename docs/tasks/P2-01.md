# P2-01 — Roofs, hops, platforms, coyote time
Owner: Sim engineer (done by the Orchestrator) · Reviewers: qa-engineer, code-reviewer · Phase: 2 · Depends on: P1-03

## Goal
Make the tops of vehicles part of play (GDD §5.8): roofs bounce, long vehicles are platforms you can drive along,
and passing over a vehicle in the air is detected for scoring.

## Context
GDD §5.5, §5.8, §9 (CLEAR, SEMI CLEARED, HOP, ROOF RIDE) · scenarios G4, G10, G11 · tuning `sim.roofs`,
`sim.jump.coyoteS`.

## Scope
`packages/sim/src/{roofs,jump,outcome,collisions,vertical,step,director,state,hash,events,index}.ts` and tests,
`packages/shared/src/events.ts`, golden replays, DECISIONS → Sim changelog.

## Out of scope
Points, meter and streaks (P2-03), presentation of rides beyond what the renderer already shows.

## Contract
New events: `rideStart` (14), `rideEnd` (15), `clear` (16); `hop` now carries the chain length and the slam flag.
Player state gains `rideSlot`, `coyoteS`, `hopChain`, `touchSeq`; traffic gains `over`. All of them are hashed.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Landing on a bus, trolleybus, semi trailer, car-carrier, sweeper or works truck rides it; driving off the end or the
   side drops the car with coyote time; a jump launches from the platform's height.
2. G4: a jump 80 ms after leaving a trailer still launches.
3. G10: a slam onto a bus roof lands as a slam and rides, no crash.
4. Roof hops count a chain that resets on the road; a slam onto a roof bounces 4.5 m high.
5. Passing over a whole vehicle in the air with no touch emits `clear`, flagged for platform vehicles (SEMI CLEARED).
